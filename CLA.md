> **This is a draft, not a reviewed legal document.** It follows the standard
> shape used by most individual CLAs (the pattern popularized by the Apache
> Software Foundation and by [project-harmony.org](https://harmony-agreements.org/)),
> adapted to name Fossferous as the receiving party. It has not been reviewed
> by a lawyer. Have one look at it — particularly the license grant in §2 and
> the patent grant in §3 — before relying on it, and before asking anyone to
> sign it.

Comment on your pull request as instructed by the CLA bot. That comment is
recorded as your signature and is required once per GitHub account, not once
per contribution.

*Modelled on the Apache ICLA and the Harmony HA-CLA-I, both freely reusable
