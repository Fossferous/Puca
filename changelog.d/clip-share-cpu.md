### Changed

- **Sharing your screen while your clip buffer is armed by hand no longer captures that screen twice.** If you armed Clips with the picker and then share the same screen or window at the same frame rate, the clip buffer now reads the share's picture instead of capturing it a second time, and keeps reading it after the share ends. Your clips are exactly as before: the same quality, the same length, nothing lost at the moment the share starts or stops. A share of another screen or window, or one smaller or at another frame rate than your clip setting, still runs beside the buffer as it did. With **Arm automatically** the buffer still captures your whole monitor itself, as before. Measured on a Ryzen 7 7800X3D: sharing costs about a third of one CPU core on an SFU call and about half a core per viewer on a direct (mesh) call, and an armed clip buffer adds about a fifth of a core either way; following the share removes the buffer's own capture of the screen. Desktop app, with the next update.
