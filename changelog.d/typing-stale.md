### Fixed

- **"Is typing…" no longer names someone who has stopped.** Coming back to the
  app on a phone after switching away, a chat often said someone was still
  typing when they were not: their message was already there, or they had
  stopped minutes ago. A message now ends its sender's "is typing" at once,
  typing that ended while the app was in the background is not shown when you
  come back (also when the app was paused and everything sent meanwhile arrives
  at once as it wakes, as on an iPhone), and someone typing in one channel is
  not shown in the next one you open. Comes with the update (no new install, no server update).
