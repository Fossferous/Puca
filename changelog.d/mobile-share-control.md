### Added

- **Let someone in a call control your phone.** While you share your whole screen from the Android app, a person watching can press **Request control** on your stream, as with a computer; you are asked on the phone every time (with a notification if you are in another app), and nothing happens until you press **Allow control** and then **Allow** in Android's own **Let "Name" control this phone?**, which nothing in Púca's page can press for you. Once allowed, their click is a tap on your screen, a drag a swipe (played as they make it, so a list scrolls and flings), a right-click a long press and the wheel a scroll; their typing goes into the text box that is open, and their stream view gains **Back**, **Home** and **Recent apps** for your phone. It works over Android's accessibility service: the first time, Púca's prompt takes you to **Settings › Accessibility › Púca remote control** to switch it on (and, on Android 13 and later, says how to **Allow restricted settings** for an app installed from the download page); switch it off there to turn it off. Púca uses it only while you have allowed someone, and does not read your screen with it. While someone is in control, an orange frame and **"Name" is controlling this phone** with a **Stop** button sit over every app, and the share's notification has **Stop control**; either Stop, Púca's own **Stop control**, locking the phone, stopping the share or leaving the call ends it at once, and it never comes back by itself, not even when you share again. It needs Android 8 or newer, the share to be of the entire screen (choose **Entire screen**: a shared app is refused where Android lets Púca tell it apart, and switching away from it ends control), the full app (Púca Lite has none of it, in its APK too) and the new Android app (APK); the person controlling needs only this update. A pending request can now be cancelled from its button (that person can then be asked again after a short wait), and one nobody answers (a Púca Lite device, say) is dropped after a little under three minutes instead of waiting for ever. Nothing changes on the server.

### Changed

- **Share Screen explains itself where it cannot work.** On a phone's browser (Android or iPhone), on an iPad asking for desktop sites and in an Android app too old to share, the voice panel's **Share Screen** used to be missing with no reason given (or, on that iPad, there and failing). It is now dimmed, and pressing it says why: browsers on Android cannot share a screen with a web page, so it points you to the Púca app for Android; Safari on iPhone and iPad cannot either before iOS 27, and Púca does not offer iOS 27's new screen sharing yet, as it has not been tried with Púca. Arrives as an ordinary update.

### Fixed

- **Stopping a screen share ends remote control of it.** Stopping your share from the voice panel's **Stop Sharing**, or the share ending by itself, used to leave the person controlling it in control: sharing again within a minute and a half gave them control of the new share without a new **Allow**. Now any way a share stops ends control at once, and a request still waiting is answered no. A second request also no longer takes over the prompt under your finger: **Allow control** waits a moment before it can be pressed for each new person asking. Needs the desktop update on the computer that shares (Púca's page there comes with the desktop app); the person controlling needs nothing new. On a phone this is part of control of a phone, which needs the new Android app (APK).
