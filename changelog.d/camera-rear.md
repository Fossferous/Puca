### Fixed

- **Flip switches to the rear camera, and you can choose any camera.** In a call on a phone, Flip opened the front camera again every time, so all it seemed to do was flicker the picture. It now switches between the front camera and the main rear one, and the round button for it sits in the corner of your own picture, where a thumb finds it. Tap your picture, or use the new arrow beside the camera button, for a menu of every camera the device has by its name (on an iPhone, each rear lens: ultra wide, telephoto and the rest), with Full screen and Turn off camera. On a computer with two webcams Flip goes to the other one, and the same menu picks among them. Your own picture is mirrored only while the camera faces you, so a rear camera shows writing the right way round. The camera you switch to is the one your camera opens next time, and **Settings → Voice & Video → Camera** chooses it before a call (it lists the cameras once Púca has been allowed to use the camera; on a phone, once your camera has been on since Púca was opened). The other camera goes out on the same encrypted connection, in ordinary calls and SFU channels alike. Also fixed: in full screen, **Turn Off Camera** was squeezed into a small red circle with its words spilling out of it. Arrives as an ordinary update (the Android app takes it over the air); nothing changes on the server.
