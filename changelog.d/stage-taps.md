### Fixed

- **Controlling a computer from a phone, taps on buttons no longer open the keyboard or zoom the picture.** In My Devices on a phone, a tap on a button beside a text box (Back beside an address bar, Send or emoji beside a message box), a long press, or any tap along the line of the last text box you typed into in Chrome, Edge or another Chromium-based app opened the keyboard, and the computer's picture zoomed in with it. The keyboard now opens by itself only when a tap puts the text cursor where you tapped: in a text box, or elsewhere in its text. A tap on a text box that already has the cursor and leaves it in place opens nothing: open the keyboard with **Keyboard** then. Closed with **Keyboard** or the phone's Back, the keyboard stays closed while you tap around in that text (it came back after five seconds, or on another line), until the cursor leaves the text and a text box takes it; in Chrome, Edge and other Chromium-based apps on a computer whose Púca is not yet updated, it still comes back after five seconds or on another line. While you type, the picture follows only the text cursor: a tap on a button, or a button taking the focus, no longer moves or zooms it. Comes with the phone's in-app update; the computer's half (Púca there no longer reports a Chromium app's hidden cursor as a text cursor) comes with its next desktop update.
