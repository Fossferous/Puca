### Security

- **Signing out takes your notifications, and what you read, with it.** Signing out does not
  restart the app, and some of your account stayed for whoever signed in next on the same
  device: your notifications (in Windows' notification centre, in an Android phone's
  notification shade, and the ones the web app shows while it is closed), the unread counts on
  the server icons and a clip approval request could still be shown to them, and the last few
  hundred messages you read, with the addresses of their link cards, were still in the app's
  memory (no screen showed them, but an attachment's key is in its link). All of it goes at a
  sign-out now, in every open Púca and Púca Notes tab. When your session ran out instead and
  somebody else signs in on the same device, it goes before they see anything too: until now
  they were shown your clip approval request and your unread counts at once, a tap on one of
  your Steam Deck notifications opened your conversation, and the people you had blocked were
  hidden from them until their own list arrived. If the app was closed without signing out, the
  notifications you were left go when somebody else signs in. Deleting your account, and
  Settings' Clear Local Storage & Reload, take your notifications away too: until now a deleted
  account's stayed for whoever signed in next. A new account's recovery code is
  shown only to that account: when the automatic sign-in after creating an account failed, the
  next person to sign in on that screen was shown it. Arrives as an ordinary update.
