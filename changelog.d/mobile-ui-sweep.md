### Fixed

- **An iPhone no longer zooms in when you tap the message box.** On a phone the message box, the password field on the sign-in page and the recovery code box behind "Forgot your password?", the emoji search and a server's description in Server Settings used slightly smaller text than iOS accepts (14 to 15 px instead of 16), so tapping one zoomed the whole app in, and it did not zoom back out. They now use 16 px on a phone, and the empty message box's "Message #channel" stays on one line instead of showing half of a second one. Arrives as an ordinary update.
- **The list that comes up when you type @ or # on a phone is as wide as the message box area.** It used to be only as wide as the text box itself (about 160 px on a phone), so a longer name was cut off mid-letter and the list could be dragged sideways. It now spans the bottom of the screen, its rows are big enough for a finger, and a name too long for the row ends in "…". Arrives as an ordinary update.
- **A message's time stays in one piece beside a long name.** On a phone, a long username squeezed the time next to it into a column ("Today / at / 17:54"), and on a narrower phone pushed it off the edge of the screen. When the name and the time do not fit on one line, the time now goes under the name, whole. Arrives as an ordinary update.
- **The checklist button at the top of a channel no longer sits in a grey box.** It was the only button in the channel's header drawn as a plain grey square (the browser's default look, on every theme); it now looks like the pin button beside it. On phones and on the desktop. Arrives as an ordinary update.
- **In Settings, tapping a setting's name turns it on or off.** Most on/off settings (Compact Mode, notifications, high contrast and the rest) answered only a press on their small tick box, which on a phone sits alone under the description and is about a third of the size of a fingertip. A tap anywhere on the setting's row, its name or its description, now ticks or unticks it; the box itself works as before. The same applies to a click on the desktop. Arrives as an ordinary update.
- **The bar along the bottom of the phone app follows your theme.** It was always dark navy, Light theme included, and the name of the tab you were on was too faint to read comfortably on every theme (high contrast too). It now uses your theme's colours, like the channel list, and every tab's name meets the usual readability standard on all eight themes. Arrives as an ordinary update.
- **In a call, the LIVE badge on someone's camera is no longer hidden under the fullscreen button.** When a person was sharing their screen with their camera on, their camera's LIVE badge and its fullscreen button were drawn in the same corner, so on a phone (where the button is always shown) the button covered the badge. The badge now sits just left of the button. Arrives as an ordinary update.
- **The call bar at the bottom of a phone no longer piles its buttons on top of each other.** Where screen sharing is offered on a phone-sized screen (the Android app, a touchscreen laptop), the share's small arrow button joined the call bar's one row, which only has room for four buttons, so the "Encrypted" badge slid under the mute button and the channel's name disappeared. That arrow now waits behind the bar's "More voice controls" button, with Share itself. Arrives as an ordinary update.
- **A long link's card shows the address on two tidy lines.** The card under a link to a site without a preview repeats the address, and a long address ran past the edge of the card and was cut off mid-word, most visibly on a phone. It now wraps inside the card and ends in "…". Arrives as an ordinary update.
- **Tasks' "Create a list…" hint no longer touches the edges of a phone's screen.** With no lists yet, the sentence under the icon ran from one edge of the screen to the other; it now has a margin and is centred. Arrives as an ordinary update.
- **On a phone, Chat in the bottom bar takes you back from Tasks.** After opening Tasks from the bottom bar, pressing Chat did nothing: Tasks stayed on the screen until you closed it with its X. Chat now goes back to the channel or conversation you last had open (or, with none, to the Friends page). Arrives as an ordinary update.
- **Settings on a phone no longer shows an "ESC" key label beside its close button.** A phone has no Esc key; the X is the button. Arrives as an ordinary update.
