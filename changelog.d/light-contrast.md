### Fixed

- **Words on the Light theme read clearly everywhere.** About 220 places on Light had words or
  icon buttons too faint against what is behind them, the worst nearly invisible: the Roles tab in
  Server Settings (dark panels under dark words), the selected Server Settings tab, the voice
  panel's clip controls and errors, unread and LIVE badges, Add Friend, avatars' initials, the
  brand-blue names and links, "Idle" and "Offline" in a profile, and the microphone test's lines in
  Settings. Every one now holds the WCAG contrast ratio (4.5:1 for words, 3:1 for icon buttons),
  with and without High Contrast, on desktop and phone. Light's reds and greens for buttons and
  badges are a little deeper. Arrives as an ordinary update.
- **The sign-in page, the update banners and a few fixed-colour controls are easier to read on
  every theme.** The sign-in page's labels, errors and subtitle, its title and Sign In button, the
  update banners, LIVE and unread badges, the role editor, the Join Server window, the remote
  control's phone buttons and the "Jump to latest" pill were drawn in the same colours on every
  theme and fell short of that ratio on all of them; they now meet it, with slightly darker reds,
  blues and purples behind white words. A Notes dialog's main button inside Púca no longer turns
  blue on blue while Notes is open. Arrives as an ordinary update.
