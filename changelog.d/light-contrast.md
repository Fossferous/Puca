### Fixed

- **Words on the Light theme read clearly.** Over 200 places on Light had words or icon buttons
  too faint against what is behind them, the worst nearly invisible: the Roles tab in Server
  Settings (dark panels under dark words), the selected Server Settings tab, the voice panel's clip
  controls and errors, unread and LIVE badges, Add Friend, avatars' initials, the brand-blue names
  and links in messages, "Idle" and "Offline" in a profile, the microphone test's lines in
  Settings, and words that were dimmed: a done task, a done calendar chip, an earlier month's days.
  Each now holds the WCAG contrast ratio (4.5:1 for words, 3:1 for icon buttons), with and without
  High Contrast, on desktop and phone. Light's reds and greens for buttons and badges are a little
  deeper, and done tasks keep a line through them in place of the fading. A role's own colour on a
  name is the server's choice and is shown as it is. Arrives as an ordinary update.
- **The sign-in page, the update banners and a few fixed-colour controls are easier to read.** The
  sign-in page's labels, errors and subtitle, its title, the update banners, LIVE and unread badges,
  the role editor, the Join Server window, the remote control's phone buttons and the "Jump to
  latest" pill were drawn in the same colours on every theme and fell short of that ratio on all of
  them; they now meet it, with slightly darker reds, blues and purples behind white words. The Sign
  In button meets it on the Dark, AMOLED and Light themes (the colour themes draw it in their own
  colour, as before). A Notes dialog's main button inside Púca no longer turns blue on blue while
  Notes is open. Arrives as an ordinary update.
