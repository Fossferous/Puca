### Fixed

- **Turning a phone that shares its screen no longer leaves viewers on a black picture.** When you turned the phone sideways (or back upright) while sharing its screen from the Android app, the people watching could get a mostly black picture with your screen as a small strip in one corner, and it stayed like that until you turned the phone again. It happened on some turns and not others. Android 14 and later work out how to fit your screen into the share from the share's size at the moment it is resized, and Púca could resize it a moment before handing over the picture of the new size, so Android fitted your turned screen to the old shape. Púca now hands over the new picture first, so every turn shows your whole screen. Needs the new Android app (APK), in Púca Lite too; nothing changes for the people watching or on the server.
